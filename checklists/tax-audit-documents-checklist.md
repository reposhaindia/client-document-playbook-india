# What documents does a CA need from clients for a tax audit?

**Short answer:** For a tax audit, a CA needs the client's complete books of accounts, bank statements for every account, the fixed asset register, stock records, GST and TDS returns with challans, loan and deposit details, related party transactions, details of cash payments and receipts, and the previous year's audit report. From Tax Year 2026-27, the tax audit report is **Form 26** under the Income-tax Act, 2025, replacing Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD. ## Tax audit documents checklist

### Books and statements

- Books of accounts: ledger, cash book, journal and trial balance
- Profit and loss account and balance sheet
- Bank statements for all accounts for the full year, with reconciliations
- Previous year's tax audit report and audited financials

### Fixed assets and stock

- Fixed asset register with additions and sales during the year
- Purchase invoices for major additions
- Stock records and the closing stock valuation method
- Stock statements submitted to banks, if any

### Taxes and compliance

- GST returns for the year, with a reconciliation of turnover to books
- TDS and TCS returns, challans and certificates
- Advance tax challans
- Details of any tax demands, notices or pending assessments

### Loans, deposits and payments

- Loans and deposits taken or repaid, with lender details
- Loans and advances given
- Payments to related parties
- Cash payments and receipts above the specified limits
- Payments to micro and small enterprises and the dates paid
- Statutory dues (PF, ESI, bonus, leave encashment) with payment dates

### Other details

- Details of expenses of a capital or personal nature
- Foreign transactions or remittances
- Changes in the nature of business, partners or shareholding

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the new tax audit form?**
Under the Income-tax Act, 2025, the tax audit report is Form 26, replacing Forms 3CA, 3CB and 3CD. Audits for years before April 2026 continue under the old forms.

**When is the tax audit due?**
For audited accounts, the return is due by 31 October following the tax year, with the audit report filed before that. Check the final notified dates.

**Why do audits get delayed?**
Mostly because documents arrive in pieces over email and WhatsApp. One request list and one upload link, with clear tracking of what is pending, saves weeks.
