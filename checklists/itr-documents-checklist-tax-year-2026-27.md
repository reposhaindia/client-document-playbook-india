# What documents should a CA collect from clients for ITR filing in 2027 (Tax Year 2026-27)?

**Short answer:** For returns filed in 2027, a CA firm should collect PAN and Aadhaar, bank account details, the salary TDS certificate (**Form 130**, which replaced Form 16), other TDS certificates (**Form 131**, which replaced Form 16A), interest and dividend details, capital gains statements, rent and home loan details, business records where relevant, and tax payment challans. The firm downloads the annual information statement (**Form 168**, which replaced Form 26AS) and the AIS itself. Send clients one checklist early and collect through a single secure link. Tools such as [Reposha](https://reposha.com) let a CA send this checklist as an upload request, so each document lands in the client's folder.

> **What changed in 2026:** The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from 1 April 2026. "Previous year" and "assessment year" are replaced by a single **tax year**. Income earned from April 2026 to March 2027 is **Tax Year 2026-27**, and its return is filed in 2027. Many form numbers changed under the new Income-tax Rules. Check the final notified forms before filing.

## Due dates for Tax Year 2026-27 returns

| Taxpayer | Due date |
|---|---|
| Individuals without business income (ITR-1, ITR-2 type returns) | 31 July 2027 |
| Business or profession, accounts not audited | 31 August 2027 |
| Accounts audited | 31 October 2027 |

Confirm due dates against the final notification for the filing season.

## Checklist for every client

- PAN and Aadhaar (Aadhaar linked to PAN)
- Mobile number and email linked to the income tax portal
- All bank accounts held during the year, with IFSC, and the account for refund
- Interest certificates: savings, fixed deposits, recurring deposits, post office schemes
- Dividend details
- Advance tax and self-assessment tax challans
- Details of any income tax notices received

The CA downloads Form 168 and the AIS from the portal and matches them with the client's documents.

## Salaried clients

- **Form 130** from each employer during the year (replaces Form 16)
- Salary slips, if there was a job change or arrears
- For the old tax regime only:
  - Rent receipts and rental agreement for HRA, and the landlord's PAN if rent is above ₹1 lakh a year
  - Proof of deductions: life insurance, PPF, ELSS, tuition fees, health insurance, donations
- Home loan interest certificate
- ESOP or RSU statements, if any

The new tax regime is the default. Old-regime deduction proofs are needed only if the client opts for the old regime.

## Clients with house property

- Rent received, tenant details and rental agreement
- Municipal tax paid
- Home loan interest certificate and principal repayment details
- Co-ownership share, if the property is jointly owned

## Clients with capital gains

- Broker's capital gains statement for shares and equity funds
- Mutual fund capital gains statement or consolidated account statement
- Property sold: sale deed, purchase deed, cost of improvement bills, stamp duty value
- Unlisted shares, bonds or gold: purchase and sale documents
- Virtual digital assets (crypto): full transaction statement from each exchange
- Reinvestment proofs for exemptions, if claimed

## Business and professional clients

- Books of accounts, or a profit and loss account and balance sheet
- Bank statements for all business accounts for the full year
- GST returns and sales and purchase registers
- TDS certificates received (**Form 131**, replaces Form 16A)
- TDS deducted and deposited by the business, with challans
- Loan statements and interest certificates
- Fixed asset purchases and sales
- For presumptive taxation: gross receipts or turnover, with the digital and cash split

## NRI clients

- Passport, with entry and exit dates to work out residential status
- NRE and NRO account statements
- Indian income: rent, interest, capital gains
- Tax Residency Certificate and declarations, if claiming treaty benefits
- Indian bank account for refund

## Senior citizen clients

- Pension certificate or pension TDS certificate
- Interest certificates from all banks and post office schemes
- Health insurance premium and medical expense proofs, if claiming under the old regime
- Copies of any declarations submitted to banks to avoid TDS

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Form 16 still issued?**
From 1 April 2026, the salary TDS certificate is Form 130 under the new Income-tax Rules. It serves the same purpose as Form 16. Returns for income earned before April 2026 still use the old forms.

**Does the client need to send Form 26AS?**
No. The CA downloads the annual information statement (Form 168, earlier Form 26AS) and the AIS directly from the portal.

**What documents are needed under the new tax regime?**
Fewer. Most deduction proofs are not needed. Income documents, TDS certificates, bank details and capital gains statements are still needed.
