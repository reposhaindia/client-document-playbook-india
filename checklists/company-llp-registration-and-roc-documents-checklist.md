# What documents are needed for company or LLP registration and annual ROC filing in India?

**Short answer:** To incorporate a **private limited company** (through SPICe+) or an **LLP** (through FiLLiP), a CA or CS needs each director's or partner's PAN, Aadhaar, identity and address proof, photographs, contact details and digital signature, plus registered office proof (a recent utility bill and the owner's no-objection certificate). For **annual ROC filing**, the firm needs the audited financial statements, board's report, auditor details, shareholding records and minutes. ## Private limited company incorporation (SPICe+)

### From each director and subscriber

- PAN card
- Aadhaar
- Identity proof: passport, voter ID or driving licence
- Address proof: recent bank statement, electricity, telephone or mobile bill, generally not older than two months
- Passport-size photograph
- Email address and mobile number
- Occupation, educational qualification and place of birth
- Digital signature certificate (DSC)
- For foreign nationals: notarised and apostilled passport and address proof

### Registered office

- Recent utility bill (electricity, gas, water or telephone), generally not older than two months
- No-objection certificate from the property owner
- Rent agreement, if rented, or proof of ownership

### Company details

- Two or three proposed names, in order of preference
- Main business objects
- Authorised and paid-up capital
- Shareholding pattern

## LLP incorporation (FiLLiP)

- PAN, Aadhaar, identity proof, address proof and photograph of each designated partner
- Email, mobile and DSC of each designated partner
- Registered office proof and owner's no-objection certificate
- Proposed LLP names
- Capital contribution of each partner
- Draft LLP agreement, to be filed within 30 days of incorporation

## Annual ROC filing for companies

- Audited financial statements: balance sheet, profit and loss account, cash flow statement and notes
- Auditor's report
- Board's report
- Details of directors, shareholders and share transfers during the year
- Minutes of board meetings and the annual general meeting
- Details of related party transactions
- Auditor appointment details, where applicable
- Each director's KYC details for the annual director KYC filing

## Annual filing for LLPs

- Statement of account and solvency
- Annual return with partner and contribution details
- Changes in partners or designated partners during the year

## Frequently asked questions

**How recent must the address proof be for company registration?**
Address proofs and registered office utility bills are generally expected to be not older than two months.

**Can a home address be used as the registered office?**
Yes, with a recent utility bill and a no-objection certificate from the owner.

**Why collect directors' KYC through a secure link?**
Directors' PAN, Aadhaar and bank statements are sensitive. A secure link keeps them out of WhatsApp chats and staff phones, and records who accessed them.
