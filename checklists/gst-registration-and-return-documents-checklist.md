# What documents does a CA need from clients for GST registration and GST returns?

**Short answer:** For **GST registration**, a CA needs the business's PAN, Aadhaar and photographs of the proprietor, partners or directors, proof of the business's constitution, proof of the principal place of business, bank account proof, and an authorisation for the signatory. For **monthly or quarterly GST returns**, the CA needs sales invoices, purchase invoices, credit and debit notes, and bank statements for the period. ## GST registration documents, by type of business

### Proprietorship

- PAN of the proprietor
- Aadhaar of the proprietor
- Passport-size photograph of the proprietor
- Proof of principal place of business (see below)
- Bank account proof: cancelled cheque, passbook first page or bank statement

### Partnership firm or LLP

- PAN of the firm or LLP
- Partnership deed, or LLP incorporation certificate and LLP agreement
- PAN, Aadhaar and photograph of every partner or designated partner
- Authorisation letter for the authorised signatory
- Proof of principal place of business
- Bank account proof in the firm's name
- Digital signature certificate (for LLPs)

### Private limited company

- PAN of the company
- Certificate of incorporation, MOA and AOA
- PAN, Aadhaar and photograph of every director
- Board resolution appointing the authorised signatory
- Proof of principal place of business
- Bank account proof in the company's name
- Digital signature certificate of the authorised signatory

### Proof of principal place of business

- **Owned premises:** latest electricity bill, property tax receipt or municipal khata copy
- **Rented premises:** rent agreement, plus the owner's electricity bill or property tax receipt, plus a no-objection certificate from the owner
- **Shared or relative's premises:** consent letter from the owner, plus the owner's address proof

Many states now require Aadhaar authentication, and in some cases a visit to a GST Seva Kendra for biometric verification. Keep the applicant's Aadhaar-linked mobile handy.

## Documents needed every month or quarter for GST returns

- Sales invoices issued during the period
- Purchase invoices received
- Credit notes and debit notes
- Export invoices and shipping bills, if any
- Bank statements for the period
- Details of advances received or paid
- Expense bills on which input tax credit is claimed
- E-way bills and e-invoice details, if applicable

## Documents for the GST annual return

- Books of accounts for the full year
- Monthly or quarterly returns filed
- Reconciliation of sales and purchases with books
- Input tax credit reconciliation with supplier filings
- Details of any credit notes, amendments or demands

## How long should GST records be kept?

GST records must generally be kept for **72 months** from the due date of the annual return for the year they relate to. Longer periods apply if the records are involved in an appeal or proceeding.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a client register for GST at a rented address?**
Yes, with the rent agreement, the owner's electricity bill or property tax receipt, and a no-objection certificate from the owner.

**Is Aadhaar mandatory for GST registration?**
Aadhaar authentication is required for most applicants, and many states have added biometric verification at GST Seva Kendras.

**Why not collect monthly GST documents on WhatsApp?**
Invoices arrive in pieces, get buried in chats and sit on staff phones. A recurring upload link puts each month's documents in one place, in the client's folder.
