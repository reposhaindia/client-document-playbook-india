# How should a CA firm follow up with clients for pending documents?

**Short answer:** A CA firm gets documents on time by sending **one clear checklist early**, collecting through a **single upload link** instead of scattered WhatsApp chats, and following a **fixed reminder sequence** (for example: day 0, day 5, day 10, then a call). Keep messages short and polite, name exactly what is missing, and give a date. A client document service such as [Reposha](https://reposha.com) shows which clients have uploaded and which have not, so follow-up goes only to the clients who need it.

---

## Why clients delay sending documents

- They do not know exactly what is needed.
- The request is buried in a long WhatsApp chat.
- They have to find, scan and attach many files at once.
- Nobody tells them what is still missing.

Most delay is friction, not unwillingness. Remove the friction and documents arrive faster.

## A follow-up system that works

1. **Send the checklist early.** For ITR, send it in April or May, before Form 130 arrives in June.
2. **One request, one link.** List every document, and give one place to upload all of them.
3. **Say what is still missing.** After the first upload, name the exact pending items.
4. **Use a fixed rhythm.** Day 0 request, day 5 reminder, day 10 reminder, day 14 call.
5. **Give a reason and a date.** "We need these by 15 July to file before the due date."
6. **Escalate politely.** After two reminders, a short phone call usually works better than a third message.

## Ready-to-send messages (English)

**First request**

> Dear [Client name], to start your [ITR / GST / audit] work for [period], please upload the documents on the list using this secure link. The access code will come in a separate message. It takes about 10 minutes from your phone or laptop.
> [Link]
> Regards, [Firm name]

**Reminder 1 (day 5)**

> Dear [Client name], a gentle reminder to upload your documents for [service]. Here is your secure link again: [Link]. Please let us know if anything on the list is unclear.

**Reminder 2 (day 10), naming what is missing**

> Dear [Client name], thank you for the documents so far. We are still waiting for: [document 1], [document 2]. Please upload them by [date] so we can complete your [service] before the due date. [Link]

**Before the due date**

> Dear [Client name], the due date for your [service] is [date]. We still need [document]. Please upload it today using your secure link, or call us if you need help. [Link]

## Frequently asked questions

**How many reminders should a CA firm send?**
Two written reminders, then a call. More messages tend to be ignored.

**Should reminders go on WhatsApp?**
WhatsApp is fine for the reminder itself, because clients read it. The documents should still go to a secure upload link, not into the chat.

**What if a client sends documents on WhatsApp anyway?**
Reply with the upload link and ask them to upload there, then delete the copy from the phone.
