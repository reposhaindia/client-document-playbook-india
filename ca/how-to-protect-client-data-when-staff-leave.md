# How should a CA firm protect client data when staff or articles leave?

**Short answer:** When a staff member or article assistant leaves, a CA firm should **remove their access to all client documents on their last day**, change any shared passwords, take them out of client WhatsApp groups, ask them to delete client files from personal devices, and check the access log for unusual activity in their final weeks. This is only fully possible when client documents sit in one system with individual logins. ## Why staff exits are the biggest data risk in a CA firm

- **Articles move on by design.** Training ends, and they join other firms or industry.
- **Staff change jobs between seasons,** often to firms serving similar clients.
- **Client files travel with them** when documents were collected on their personal WhatsApp or email.
- **Nobody knows what they hold.** Without an access record, the firm cannot tell which client files a former employee saw or kept.

Most exits are friendly, and most people mean no harm. The risk is not bad intent. It is copies of confidential documents sitting on a phone the firm no longer controls.

## Exit checklist for CA firms

### Before the last day

1. **List the clients they handled,** and assign each one to another staff member.
2. **Review the access log** for their last 30 days. Look for bulk downloads or clients they did not work on.
3. **Collect pending documents** from their chats and email, and move them into the right client folders.

### On the last day

4. **Remove their access** to the client document system, accounting software, the income tax and GST portals used for the firm, and shared drives.
5. **Change shared passwords** they knew, including office Wi-Fi and any shared portal logins.
6. **Remove them from client WhatsApp groups** and the firm's internal groups.
7. **Collect firm devices,** laptops and pen drives.
8. **Ask them to delete client files** from their personal phone, gallery and cloud backups, and to confirm in writing.

### After they leave

9. **Tell key clients** about their new point of contact.
10. **Recheck the log** after a week to make sure no access continued.

## Prevent the problem before anyone joins

- **Confidentiality clause.** Include it in the appointment letter for staff and in the articleship terms.
- **No client documents on personal WhatsApp.** Collect through the firm's system from day one.
- **Access client by client.** A new joiner should see only the clients they work on, never the whole practice.
- **Individual logins.** Never share one office account. It makes removal and tracking impossible.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can a firm stop a former article from keeping client files on their phone?**
Not completely, once files are on a personal device. That is why prevention matters: if documents are collected into the firm's system instead of personal chats, there is far less to leave with.

**How quickly should access be removed?**
On the last working day, before they leave the office. Delays of even a few days are a common gap.

**What if a former employee contacts clients afterwards?**
Your confidentiality clause and client communication matter here. The access log also helps you show which files the person did and did not access while employed.
