# How should a CA firm store client documents securely?

**Short answer:** A CA firm should store client documents in **one secure system**, not on staff phones, personal email or loose pen drives. Each client should have a separate folder, each staff member their own login, and access should be given client by client. The system should keep a record of every file opened, store data in India, and back it up. ## The five rules of secure client document storage

### 1. One place, not five

Documents should live in one system. Most data leaks in small firms come from copies: a statement on a phone, the same file in two inboxes, a pen drive in a drawer.

### 2. Separate folders, separate access

Every client gets their own folder. Staff and articles see only the clients they work on. Partners see everything.

### 3. Every person has their own login

Shared office logins make it impossible to know who did what, and impossible to remove one person's access.

### 4. Every access is recorded

The firm should be able to answer, in a minute: who opened this client's documents, and when?

### 5. Data stays in India, with backups

Client tax and financial data is sensitive. Keeping it in India with trusted providers, and backed up, protects both the client and the firm.

## Practical security steps for any CA firm

1. **Turn on two-step verification** for every account that holds client data, starting with the owner.
2. **Do not save client documents to phone galleries.** Turn off auto-download of WhatsApp media on office phones.
3. **Lock office computers** when unattended, and use separate Windows logins for each person.
4. **Avoid sending documents as email attachments** inside the firm. Share access to the folder instead.
5. **Encrypt laptops** that leave the office.
6. **Review who has access** every quarter, and after any staff change.
7. **Delete what you no longer need,** once legal retention periods are over.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it safe for a CA firm to store client documents in the cloud?**
Yes, if the service encrypts data, limits access per user, keeps access logs and stores data with trusted providers. A well-run cloud service is usually safer than documents spread across office PCs and phones.

**Should client data be stored in India?**
Keeping client data in India is the simplest choice for Indian firms, and it is what many clients expect for their tax and financial records.

**How long should a CA firm keep client documents?**
It depends on the law behind each service. GST records, for example, must generally be kept for 72 months from the due date of the annual return. Set a retention period for each service in your firm's policy.
