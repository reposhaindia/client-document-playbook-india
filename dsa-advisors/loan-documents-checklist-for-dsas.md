# What documents should a DSA or loan agent collect for a home loan or business loan in India?

**Short answer:** A DSA (direct selling agent) or loan advisor typically collects KYC (PAN, Aadhaar, photographs), income proof (salary slips, Form 130 and bank statements for salaried applicants, or income tax returns, financials and GST returns for self-employed applicants), and for home loans, the property documents. Exact lists vary by lender. Because customers share Aadhaar, PAN and bank statements, DSAs should collect them through a secure upload link instead of WhatsApp. [Reposha](https://reposha.com) gives each customer a private link and a secure folder, with a log of every file opened.

## KYC documents for every applicant and co-applicant

- PAN card
- Aadhaar
- Passport-size photographs
- Address proof, if different from Aadhaar
- Signature proof, if required by the lender

## Salaried applicants

- Last 3 to 6 months' salary slips
- Salary TDS certificate for the last 1 to 2 years (Form 130, earlier Form 16)
- Bank statements for the last 6 months, showing salary credits
- Employment letter or ID card
- Existing loan statements, if any

## Self-employed and business applicants

- Income tax returns with computation for the last 2 to 3 years
- Audited or CA-certified financial statements for the last 2 to 3 years
- Business bank statements for the last 12 months
- GST registration and recent GST returns
- Business proof: GST certificate, Udyam registration, shop establishment licence or partnership deed
- Existing loan statements and sanction letters

## Home loan property documents

- Agreement for sale or allotment letter
- Title documents and chain of title
- Approved building plan
- Builder's no-objection certificate and project approvals, for new projects
- Occupancy certificate, for ready properties
- Society no-objection certificate and share certificate, for resale flats
- Receipts of payments made to the builder or seller

## Frequently asked questions

**Why shouldn't DSAs collect documents on WhatsApp?**
Customers' Aadhaar, PAN and bank statements end up on the agent's phone and backups, often mixed with personal chats, with no record of who saw them. A secure link keeps each customer's documents in one place.

**Do all lenders ask for the same documents?**
No. The core KYC and income documents are similar, but each lender adds its own requirements. Confirm the lender's list before collecting.

**How long should a DSA keep customer documents?**
Only as long as needed for the application and as agreed with the lender. Under the DPDP Act, personal data should be deleted once its purpose is over, unless a law requires otherwise.
