# What documents should an advocate collect from a client at intake in India?

**Short answer:** At intake, an advocate or law firm should collect the client's identity and contact details, a conflict check, a written chronology of the matter, and every document connected to the dispute: agreements, notices, correspondence, earlier court papers and proof of payments or property. Each matter type has its own core documents, listed below. Firms should receive these through a secure upload link rather than WhatsApp, because case papers are highly confidential. ## Intake documents for every matter

- Client's full name, address, phone and email
- Identity proof: PAN, Aadhaar, passport or voter ID
- For companies and firms: incorporation documents and an authorisation or board resolution
- Conflict check: names of all opposite parties and related parties
- Written chronology of events, in the client's own words, with dates
- All documents the client holds about the matter
- Any notices, summons or orders already received
- Details of any earlier advocate and earlier proceedings
- Signed vakalatnama, once engaged
- Engagement letter or fee terms

## Core documents by matter type

### Property and title disputes

- Sale deed, gift deed, will or partition deed
- Chain of title documents
- Property card, land record extracts and mutation entries
- Encumbrance certificate
- Property tax receipts and utility bills
- Approved building plan and occupancy certificate, where relevant
- Any agreement to sell, receipts and payment proofs

### Cheque bounce (Negotiable Instruments Act, Section 138)

- Original cheque and the bank's return memo
- Copy of the legal notice sent, with postal or courier proof and date of delivery
- Reply to the notice, if any
- Underlying invoice, loan agreement or proof of liability
- Bank statement showing presentation and dishonour

### Family and matrimonial matters

- Marriage certificate or proof of marriage
- Identity and address proofs of both parties
- Children's birth certificates and school details
- Income proofs: salary slips, tax returns, bank statements
- Property and asset details
- Messages, emails or other evidence relied on

### Recovery and commercial disputes

- Contract, purchase orders and invoices
- Statements of account and ledger extracts
- Correspondence and email trail
- Demand notices and replies
- Proof of delivery or performance

### Employment matters

- Appointment letter and employment contract
- Salary slips and bank statements
- Termination or disciplinary letters
- Company policies relied on
- Relevant emails and messages

## Frequently asked questions

**Why should advocates not collect case papers on WhatsApp?**
Case papers sit on the receiving associate's phone and personal backups, with no record of who saw them. For confidential matters, a secure folder with access limited to the matter team is far safer.

**Should a law firm keep originals or copies?**
Collect scanned copies for preparation. Keep originals with the client, or record their custody carefully, until they are needed in court.

**What if the client has documents in a regional language?**
Collect them as they are, and arrange translations where they will be filed.
