# How should advocates and law firms in India protect client data and confidentiality?

**Short answer:** Advocates in India owe clients a duty of confidentiality under the Bar Council of India Rules, and advocate-client communications are protected under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. In practice, a law firm protects client data by keeping case papers in one secure system instead of WhatsApp and email, giving associates access matter by matter, recording who opened which file, and removing access when an associate leaves. ## Where law firms are most exposed

- **Case papers on associates' phones.** Clients send documents on WhatsApp, and they stay in phone galleries and personal backups.
- **Shared email and drives.** Everyone in the office can browse every matter.
- **No record of access.** If a document leaks, the firm cannot tell who opened it.
- **Associates moving on.** Documents in personal chats leave with them.

The real risk is not people switching sides. It is confidential client data leaking and being misused, often by accident.

## How to protect client confidentiality in practice

1. **Collect documents in one place.** Use a secure upload link for each client instead of WhatsApp or email.
2. **Give access by matter.** An associate should see only the matters they work on.
3. **Use individual logins.** Never share one office account.
4. **Keep an access log.** Know who opened each document, and when.
5. **Protect the senior's account** with two-step verification.
6. **Remove access the day an associate or clerk leaves.**
7. **Avoid forwarding documents by email** inside the firm. Share access instead.
8. **Delete what you no longer need,** once the matter and any limitation period are over.

## Confidentiality duties at a glance

| Source | What it means for the firm |
|---|---|
| Bar Council of India Rules | Duty not to disclose client confidences |
| Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 | Advocate-client communications are privileged |
| Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 | Firm must protect clients' personal data with reasonable security safeguards; main duties apply from 13 May 2027 |

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the DPDP Act apply to advocates?**
Yes. A law firm that collects clients' personal data decides how it is used and stored, so it acts as a data fiduciary. The main duties apply from 13 May 2027.

**Is WhatsApp safe for receiving case documents?**
Messages are encrypted in transit, but files then sit on the receiving phone and its backups, with no access control and no record of who saw them later.

**How can a senior advocate know which associate opened a client's file?**
Only if documents sit in a system with individual logins and an access log. WhatsApp, email and shared drives with one login cannot show this.
